Privacy Policy

Updated November 25, 2026

Life Email is a personal household tool operated by Jarred Mosher.

Information accessed and used

When authorized, Life Email accesses Google Calendar information to prepare household briefings and uses Gmail to send those briefings. It may also process tasks, weather forecasts, and devotional source material supplied for the reports. Access is limited to the permissions granted by the account holder.

Storage and sharing

Application code is maintained in a private repository. Account credentials and authorization tokens are kept separately from public website files and source control. The deployed application uses Railway for hosting. Report contents are transmitted through Google to the configured recipients and their email providers. These public information pages do not display account data.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train generalized AI models. If an AI service is added for devotional writing, calendar, email, and task data will not be sent to it unless this policy is updated and the affected account holder explicitly authorizes that use.

Retention and control

Authorization tokens are retained while the connection is needed. Delivered reports remain in the sender's and recipients' mailboxes under their email providers' retention settings. Account holders can revoke Google access through their Google Account's third-party connections settings and request deletion of application-held data.

Google API data

Life Email's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
